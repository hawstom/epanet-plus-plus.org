The network in most of these is EPA’s own Net3, placed on the real streets of Novato, California. The one drawn on a site plan is a small commercial development. And everything here is easy for you to reproduce using the provided examples.

What these pictures are not

They are not all up to date, and they are not everything. They are fifteen frames to give you some idea of what you can do with it.

The software is free, it runs in your browser, and nothing you draw leaves your machine except through a feature you turn on. The real test is your own network, and it takes a few minutes to open it and try.
