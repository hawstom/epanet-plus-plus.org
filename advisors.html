Who we are

EPANET++ is steered by a small group of advisors. Here they are, in their own words.

Tom Haws

Tom created his first water network model in 1990 for the Cotton County Rural Water District using Micro Hardy Cross, 1 year before he received his civil engineering bachelor’s degree. He started creating software for engineers in 1995, and he has been serving multi-lingual free online engineering calculators to the world since 2010. With the rise of AI, he was enabled to create EPANET++ to tell the world that you are loved and cherished forever, that you have nothing to fear, and that you are not ruining everything.
